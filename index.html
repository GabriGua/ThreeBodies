What is this?

The three-body problem is a fascinating physics problem that aims to study
the trajectories resulting from the motion of three celestial bodies in
the universe, using Newton's law of universal gravitation.

Where did it all begin?

It started, with the study of how two bodies behave in space,
and it was not hard to see that, regardless of their motion and
initial position, it was possible to derive a closed-form analytical solution
that made it possible to predict how the simulation would evolve.

From Two to Three bodies

However, when a third body is added, the orbits, which were previously stable
and predictable, take on chaotic and unpredictable motions, often leading
one planet to be ejected from the system.

But in the same way, it has been possible to find stable orbits under certain conditions,
and to this day the community is still searching for stable orbits in a universe of chaos.
